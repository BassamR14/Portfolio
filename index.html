About Me

I'm studying front end because I've always liked the mix of logic and design — taking something I've coded and turning it into something people can actually see and interact with. I like that there's always something new to learn, and front end moves fast enough that it never really feels stale.

I'm also the kind of person who can get completely caught up in a random problem or idea and end up down a rabbit hole for hours, usually coming out of it having learned a bunch of things I wasn't even looking for.

I think communication is just as important as the technical side of things. Understanding what's needed, asking questions, and working well with others are things I really value.
